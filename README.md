# 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze Amazon product sales performance.

The dashboard transforms sales and product-level data into an executive-friendly analytical view covering:

* 💰 Revenue performance
* 📦 Product sales
* ⭐ Customer reviews
* 📈 Monthly sales trends
* 📅 Weekly sales trends
* 🏷️ Category-level performance
* 🏆 Top-performing products

The objective is to provide a centralized dashboard where users can quickly monitor sales KPIs and drill into product and category performance.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of product and transaction data. Without a centralized analytical dashboard, it can be difficult to answer important questions such as:

* How much revenue has been generated?
* How are sales performing compared with previous periods?
* Which product categories contribute the most sales?
* Which products are the top revenue generators?
* Which products receive the highest number of customer reviews?
* How does sales performance change throughout the month?
* Which weeks show stronger or weaker sales activity?

This project addresses these questions through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

# 🧮 KPI Framework

The dashboard is structured around several important business metrics.

### YTD Sales

Measures sales generated from the beginning of the selected year through the current reporting period.

### QTD Sales

Measures sales generated within the selected quarter.

### YTD Product Sold

Tracks the quantity of products sold during the year-to-date period.

### YTD Reviews

Tracks the number of customer reviews accumulated during the year-to-date period.

These KPIs provide complementary views of **revenue, sales volume, and customer engagement**.
